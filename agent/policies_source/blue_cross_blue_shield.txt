Plan Name: BCBS PPO Plus 2025
Effective Date: January 1, 2025
Document Version: v1.0
Applies To: In-network and out-of-network benefits unless otherwise stated.

1 Overview of Covered Benefits
This Policy provides coverage for Medically Necessary services that are not otherwise
excluded. Covered Benefits include, but are not limited to:
- Physician office visits and specialist consultations
- Laboratory and diagnostic testing
- Specialty and biologic drug therapy (Formulary-based, subject to Prior Authorization)
- Emergency services

2 Member Eligibility and Plan Status
Benefits are payable only if the member is enrolled and the Plan is active on the date
of service. If the member's Plan is inactive at the time services are rendered, the
claim will be denied. Denial reason: "Plan inactive at time of service."

3 Prior Authorization (Pre-Approval)
3.1 Services Requiring Prior Authorization
The following services require Prior Authorization:
- Specialty drugs listed as "PA Required" on the Formulary, including biologic
  injections for severe asthma
- Elective inpatient admissions
- CT and MRI scans (except emergency)
- Durable medical equipment (DME) exceeding $500

3.2 Documentation Required
Prior Authorization requests must include: (1) ordering provider's clinical note,
(2) relevant diagnosis code(s), (3) prior treatment history (if applicable), and
(4) expected service date and location. If documentation is missing, the request will
be pended until complete information is received -- no approval or denial can be
issued until it is.

4 Medical Necessity Standard
A service is considered Medically Necessary if it is consistent with generally accepted
standards of medical practice, clinically appropriate in type, frequency, and setting,
not primarily for convenience of the member or provider, and the most cost-effective
option among clinically comparable alternatives.

5 Coverage for Specialty and Biologic Drug Therapy
5.1 Biologic Injection Coverage (Asthma)
A biologic injection for asthma is covered when all of the following are met:
- Diagnosis of severe persistent asthma (e.g., J45.40 or related codes); and
- Documented inadequate control on standard inhaled corticosteroid therapy; and
- Prior Authorization has been approved, supported by a clinical note.
Prior Authorization requests missing the ordering provider's clinical note cannot be
evaluated for medical necessity and must be pended for documentation.

5.2 Preventive and Screening Services
Routine screening laboratory panels (e.g., a lipid panel performed without symptoms or
a qualifying diagnosis) are classified as preventive screening and are excluded from
standard coverage under this plan. Denials for services classified as preventive
screening may only be overturned on appeal if new clinical documentation is submitted
establishing a qualifying diagnosis or symptomatic indication; an appeal letter alone,
without new clinical documentation, does not meet this bar and the original denial
should be upheld.

6 Exclusions
The Plan does not cover: cosmetic or reconstructive procedures not required by trauma
or congenital anomaly; services deemed not Medically Necessary; experimental or
investigational treatments; services for which required Prior Authorization was not
obtained; routine preventive screening as described in 5.2; duplicate claims for the
same service date and provider unless corrected and resubmitted.

7 Claims Processing Rules
7.1 Duplicate Claims
A claim is considered duplicate if Member ID, Provider NPI, Date of service, CPT /
procedure code, and billed amount all match. Duplicate claims are denied with reason:
"Duplicate claim submission."

7.2 Coding Consistency
Claims are routed for coding review if the diagnosis and procedure codes are clinically
inconsistent based on standard coding guidelines. Such claims must not be approved
until coding review is complete.

8 Appeals
Members and providers have the right to appeal a denial within 180 days. Appeals must
include a written appeal request, the denial letter or claim reference number, and any
new clinical documentation relevant to the denial reason. Appeals without new or
relevant clinical documentation may be upheld.

9 Contact Information
For questions about benefits, Prior Authorization, or claim status, contact BCBS Member
Support at 1-800-555-0133 or via the Member Portal.
